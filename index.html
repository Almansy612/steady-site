Steady

Track every lift.

An iPhone app for logging the weight you lift and seeing it go up. Your workouts stay on your phone.

What it does

  • Log each exercise's sets, weights and reps in a few taps.
  • See whether each lift is going up, with a trend for every exercise.
  • Premium adds a suggested next weight for each lift, with the reason why.

Private by design

Your workouts are stored only on your iPhone and never uploaded. An account is optional. Read the privacy policy.
