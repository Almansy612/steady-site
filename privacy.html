Privacy Policy

Steady, the weight-progression tracker for iPhone. Effective 4 October 2026.

In short

  • Your workouts stay on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in, we store your name, email address and an account ID, nothing else.
  • The Assistant runs on your iPhone. Your questions aren't sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Steady is made by Abdallah Almansy, an independent developer ("we"). If you have a question about your data, email steedy.help@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Everything you log in Steady is stored only on your iPhone: your workouts, sets, weights and reps, your exercises and day types, how a session felt, and the setup answers you give (such as your unit, goal, experience, age range, sex and build). Your Assistant conversations are also stored only on your iPhone. We never receive any of it.

  • Backups you make. If you export a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it. We don't receive a copy.
  • iCloud Backup. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud, Apple may include Steady's data in that backup under Apple's own terms. We can't read it.
  • Deleting. Deleting the app deletes this data from your iPhone.

The Assistant

The Assistant uses Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) to answer questions about your training. Your questions, your logs and its answers are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Optional account

You can use Steady without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, our sign-in provider, Supabase, stores:

  • your name, as Apple or Google shares it with us;
  • your email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address that hides your real one);
  • which service you signed in with, and an account ID.

We use this only to identify your account. Your workouts are not linked to it and stay on your iPhone. Like any web service, Supabase's servers keep short-lived technical logs of sign-in requests, such as IP addresses. Signing in is also handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account: in Steady, go to Preferences and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account from Supabase. Your workouts stay on your iPhone.

App updates

Steady checks Expo's update service for small app updates. That request sends technical information, such as the app version and the device platform, along with your IP address, as any internet request does. It doesn't include your workouts or your account.

Apple and TestFlight

If you share app analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports. If you test Steady through TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports and feedback you choose to send. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking tools inside the app.
  • We never sell or share your data with data brokers.

Your rights

You can see and change everything you've logged in the app itself, and delete it by deleting the app. You can delete your account at any time in Preferences. To ask what account data we hold, or for anything else about your data, email steedy.help@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Steady is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. Using the app doesn't require an account.

Changes to this policy

If Steady starts handling data differently, for example when paid subscriptions are added, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
